Various Units in our School

Schools are not just centers of academic learning but also hubs for character building, leadership development, and community service. These programs encourage students to engage in activities that foster teamwork, community service, and personal development. By participating in these units, students not only build character but also learn to contribute positively to society, preparing them to become well-rounded and responsible citizens.

Student Police Cadet(SPC)

Our Lady of Lourde is blessed to have an SPC unit from the year 2014 with the motto "We leam to Serve". inculcates in our students a sense of discipline, responsibility, Social awareness and legal literacy. It pays attention in the formation of character and behaviour.

National Service Scheme(NSS)

National Service Scheme functions here impeccably. It has undertaken many socially useful activities from its inception. Under the efficient leadership of Smt. Priya Thomas, the unit has achieved many awards and recognitions.

Scout and Guide

Scout and Guide units aim to support young people in their physical mental and spiritual development. It promotes and preserves the basic culture of school campus.

National Cadet Corps(NCC)

We have a self financing NCC unit in this school from the academic year 2020-21. This unit is working under the guidance of 17 Kerala battalion NCC Pala under Kottayam Group..

Souhrida Club

The objective of Souhrida Club is to improve the physical academic social and interpersonal skills of the adolescents and lead them towards successful adulthood.

Career Guidence Unit

There is an active Career Guidance Unit in our school. The objective of the unit is to improve the career prospects of second year students. It conducts periodic orientation classes for the students and their parents.

About Us

OLL HSS UZHAVOOR was established in 1919 and it is managed by the Pvt. Aided. It is located in Rural area. It is located in RAMAPURAM block of KOTTAYAM district of Kerala. Established with a vision to nurture young minds, the school blends tradition with modern educational practices to empower students for a bright future.
