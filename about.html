About Us

OLL HSS UZHAVOOR was established in 1919 and it is managed by the Pvt. Aided. Oll High School & Higher Secondary School, Uzhavoor, stands as a beacon of academic excellence and holistic development in the heart of Kerala. Established with a vision to nurture young minds, the school blends tradition with modern educational practices to empower students for a bright future. With a dedicated team of educators, state-of-the-art facilities, and a vibrant campus life, Oll HSS fosters an environment of curiosity, creativity, and character building. Committed to academic rigor and co-curricular excellence, the institution continues to shape responsible citizens and lifelong learners, upholding its legacy of quality education and community engagement.It is located in Rural area. It is located in RAMAPURAM block of KOTTAYAM district of Kerala.
